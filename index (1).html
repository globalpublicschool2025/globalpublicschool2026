About Our School

Shaping Future Leaders with Quality Education

Global Public School, located at Nayakabandha, Bidubazar, Bahanaga, is committed to providing excellence in education following the CBSE curriculum. We nurture students from Nursery to Class 8 with a holistic approach to learning.

Our school focuses on academic excellence, character building, and extracurricular development to create well-rounded individuals ready to face the challenges of the modern world.
